`The Internet Used to Be Fun, But Now it's Just a Boring Place <the-internet-isnt-fun-anymore.html>`_
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

.. image:: {static}images/boring.png
   :alt: bored internet user

The internet is not about content anymore. Instead it is now about money and content creators,
who seem to be in control, since they determine what is shoved down our throats by the so called
algorithms. This article by Kyle Chayka sums it up all.

   The social-media Web as we knew it, a place where we consumed the posts of our fellow-humans and
   posted in return, appears to be over.

   -- By Kyle Chayka
