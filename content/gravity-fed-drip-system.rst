**Fabiola F1 Hybrid cabbages under drip irrigation**

As someone who spends most of their day on a computer, there is nothing that I love more than getting outside
and getting my hands dirty. That's why I was so excited to install a DIY gravity-fed drip irrigation system
for my 300 Fabiola F1 cabbage plants.

Using a 100 litre drum tank, I was able to create a simple and effective irrigation system that is both
cost-effective and water-efficient. By using a gravity-fed system, I was able to avoid the need for a costly
and energy-intensive pump, while still providing ample water to my plants.

One thing that really excites me about this system is the fact that it allows me to grow my crops without
having to constantly water the weeds and other unwanted plants that inevitably grow in any garden. By using
drip tape with flat emitters and 30cm spacing, I was able to deliver the perfect amount of water directly to
the plants, ensuring that they thrive while minimizing water waste.

.. image:: {static}images/mygarden.jpg
   :alt: image of vegies

This system is particularly important to me given the water problems that we face here in Harare. With climate
change making it increasingly difficult to rely on rain-fed agriculture alone, I believe that DIY irrigation
systems like this one are going to become increasingly important for anyone looking to grow their own food.

In the end, I'm thrilled with the results of my DIY gravity-fed drip irrigation system. I've been able to
grow beautiful, healthy cabbage plants without having to constantly water them by hand, and I've done my
part to save water in the process. Plus, there's no better feeling than getting outside and getting your
hands dirty when you've spent all day in front of a computer - so I'm already looking forward to my next
DIY gardening project!
